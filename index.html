ClickMomentz

A live AI photo platform for large events, at Mastermine Technologies.

Guests at a large event take one selfie and find every photo they appear in. In a team of six engineers, I build the face-recognition pipeline and the engine that culls thousands of shots down to the best ones.

  • Face recognition with InsightFace: SCRFD detection and 512-number ArcFace embeddings on ONNX Runtime, searched by cosine similarity in FAISS. About 95% matching accuracy from a single selfie, with near-instant search.

Vehicle re-identification across cameras

My M.Tech thesis, with a paper accepted at IJITCS.

Finding the same vehicle across a city's cameras usually means comparing it with every candidate. I added a physics-aware Temporal Consistency Module to the TransReID transformer. It uses the camera network's layout and realistic travel times to rule out matches that could not physically happen, before any visual comparison.

  • On VeRi-776 it removes 99.7% of impossible candidate pairs (373 of 126,063 survive), so search runs 338 times faster, with no retraining.
  • Accuracy holds: mAP rises from 82.1% to 82.5%, and Rank-1 stays at 97.4%.
  • Paper: "Physics-Aware Rule-Based Temporal Filtering for Scalable Vehicle Re-Identification", with Jithin Mathews. Accepted at IJITCS (MECS Press).
